# Hard numbers unlock Pencraft's next skills

Pencraft's design library should grow first where a model cannot guess the answer: measured component anatomy, chart anatomy, fixed format sizes with safe zones, and platform token tables. The library today is four skills in 16 files and 2,840 lines (liquid-glass, ui-components, devices, logos), and every request with "a look of its own" that is not a logo, plus every named design system other than shadcn, Radix and Base UI, falls back to the general guide alone (`app/src/skills.rs:31-63`, `app/skills/ui-components/SKILL.md:3`). The ranked list below puts five new reference files inside ui-components first, because they cost nothing in the skills index and are tested automatically in light and dark; then three new skills (charts, formats, material) whose numbers were read at primary sources; then guide sections, review checks and templates; then craft skills (landing pages, slides, diagrams, icons, illustration) whose rules are sound but whose numbers are thinner. Before any of that, seven things in the existing library are out of date or self-defeating, the worst being that ui-components still tells models the engine has no shadow spread and no component states, both of which shipped. Eleven candidates are limited by engine features (no tabular figures, no truncation, dashed strokes only on connectors, Lucide as the only icon set, one-page PDF) or by licences (Apple's and Microsoft's system fonts cannot be bundled). Sourcing is uneven and the report grades it per item: Material 3, Fluent, shadcn and store-asset figures are primary; social safe zones, slide margins and illustration rules are not. One caveat applies throughout: no vendor has published a measured evaluation of which bundled knowledge improves output most, so the ranking rests on demand evidence, on how unguessable the numbers are, and on cost, not on tested results ([Anthropic blog](https://claude.com/blog/improving-frontend-design-through-skills)).

## Seven corrections come before any new skill

The first correction is inside ui-components itself. Its "what Pencraft cannot draw" table (`app/skills/ui-components/SKILL.md:190-200`) says "a shadow that spreads" and "variants and states on a component" are missing, and `references/tokens.md:97` repeats that shadows "have no spread, which Pencraft does not have". Both are wrong since versions 0.3.21 and 0.3.8: shadow spread is read on drop and inner shadows (`app/MECHANICS.md:71-72`) and components carry real `states` (`app/MECHANICS.md:13`, `app/src/agent.rs:154-159`). The skill still builds states "as overrides on an instance" (`SKILL.md:108`) and no skill contains a `"states"` key. The effect is that models are steered away from ring-shaped focus indicators and from the one engine feature that prompts more than a resting state. The fix is to rewrite the state recipes onto real component states, replace the backing-rectangle focus ring with a zero-offset spread shadow, and correct the table; because every ui-components block is loaded and reviewed in Light and Dark by a unit test (`app/src/skills.rs:345-376`), the rewrite verifies itself. Masks also landed (0.3.11, `app/MECHANICS.md:190`), so the older advice to fake vignettes with page-colour overlays is obsolete too. Whether a shape can carry several strokes of different widths, and whether `oklch()` input is accepted, were not verified and should be checked before those two rows are changed.

The second correction is in the format guide. Its Effects section lists colour, offset and blur only (`app/src/agent.rs:122-125`), so spread is taught nowhere; shader effects exist (`app/MECHANICS.md:76-77`) and the word "shader" does not occur in the guide; `context`, `flipX` and `flipY` exist in the model and are unnamed (`app/crates/pen-doc/src/model.rs:233,248-251`).

The third is a trap the earlier fidelity work created. `names_a_reference` recognises "material design", "material 3", "fluent", "carbon design", "human interface", "bootstrap", "ant design" and others (`app/src/craft.rs:289-312`), and a named reference switches off the dealt directions and the default-face remark and tells the model to keep "that reference's face, palette, measures and states" (`craft.rs:28-34`). Yet "Material" has zero hits in ui-components, and `references/styles.md` covers only shadcn/ui, Radix Themes, Base UI and Sonner, Vaul, cmdk. A request for Material 3 or Windows 11 therefore removes Pencraft's originality machinery while supplying no numbers; the model draws the system from memory. Until the platform skills below exist, the injected note should say plainly that no skill covers the reference and that measures come from the model's memory and need checking.

The fourth concerns devices. The skill's one template is the iPhone 16 and liquid-glass sizes its phone at 393 x 852 (`app/skills/liquid-glass/references/tokens.md:108-116`); the iPhone 17 and 17 Pro are 402 x 874 points, the iPhone Air 420 x 912 and the 17 Pro Max 440 x 956, by third-party measurement ([Use Your Loaf](https://useyourloaf.com/blog/iphone-17-screen-sizes/)). The notes did not read the skill's table row by row, so the action is to confirm those rows exist and decide whether the default template should move. The description promises "an Android phone or an iPad", but both are numbers in prose with "no template for these yet" (`app/skills/devices/SKILL.md:127-137`).

The fifth is liquid-glass. It is the only skill without a "Do not use for" clause (`app/skills/liquid-glass/SKILL.md:3`), which matters once a plain Apple or Material skill sits beside it. Apple previewed iOS 27 and macOS 27 on 8 June 2026 with Liquid Glass refinements (a transparency slider, more consistent window corners, a uniform toolbar) ([MacRumors](https://macrumors.com/2026/06/10/how-liquid-glass-is-changing-in-ios-27)); the notes did not confirm the public release or any changed metric, so this is a re-check, not a known error. macOS 26 window corners are reported by the community at about 26 points for toolbar windows and 16 for title-bar-only windows, against about 10 before ([MacRumors forum](https://forums.macrumors.com/threads/want-the-bigger-window-corner-radius-back-come-inside.2484898/)); the skill's desktop-window line should be compared with that.

The sixth is the vocabulary in the general guide. Section 6 of CRAFT still defines only bolder, quieter, simpler, polish and review (`craft.rs:201-216`); "denser", "add states", "dark mode", "make it accessible" and "options" remain undefined, and "dark mode" and "accessib" have zero hits. This was planned on 14 November and is open. The planned 25-line "standard controls" block is also absent from CRAFT, so a model that never opens the skill still has no control ladder.

The seventh is that lists of tells age. Anthropic's current frontend-design skill now names its own earlier remedies as tells: cream background with a serif and a terracotta accent, near-black with one acid-green accent, broadsheet hairlines ([frontend-design SKILL.md](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/anthropics/skills/main/skills/frontend-design/SKILL.md)). Pencraft deals from five grounds and six lead faces, among them "light warm paper" and "serif with character", and its 24 worlds include a broadsheet front page (`craft.rs:221-265`). The dealing mechanism is the right answer to convergence, since Anthropic's own write-up says models re-converge on new favourites unless told to vary ([Anthropic blog](https://claude.com/blog/improving-frontend-design-through-skills)), but the specific combination of warm paper ground and characterful serif is now the most recognisable generated look and deserves a guard. Two content-level tells from 2026 critiques are also missing from CRAFT's visual list: the mad-lib headline ("The AI-powered X for modern Y") and the stock section order ([Superdesign](https://superdesign.dev/blog/fix-generic-ai-landing-page)).

One smaller point belongs here. ui-components mentions "chart colours of the neutral theme" (`references/styles.md:64`). shadcn's current neutral `chart-1` to `chart-5` are five greys, identical in light and dark ([themes.ts](https://github.com/shadcn-ui/ui/blob/main/apps/v4/registry/themes.ts)); the multi-hue orange, teal and navy set most screenshots show is the legacy 2025 palette. Copied blindly, the lightest grey on a white card and the darkest on a dark card fall well under the 3:1 that WCAG asks of chart marks ([W3C Understanding 1.4.11](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Understanding/non-text-contrast.html)); the low ratios are the note-taker's calculation, not a sourced figure.

## The ranked list: sixteen additions, graded by how well their numbers are sourced

Three facts about how skills are wired set the order. Every skill description (40 to 1,024 characters) is sent with every chat brief and is the only routing signal for outside clients, so each new skill has a standing cost (`app/src/skills.rs:99-112`, `app/src/chat.rs:98`). A reference file inside an existing skill costs no index tokens, and in ui-components it is covered by the existing light-and-dark review test. And the published evidence, thin as it is, favours short skills loaded on demand by task type over one large always-loaded guide ([Anthropic blog](https://claude.com/blog/improving-frontend-design-through-skills)). The ranking therefore weighs demand, how unusable the output is without the numbers, how well the numbers are sourced, and index cost.

Sourcing grades used below: **A** means read at the primary source (token files, source code, official specification pages); **B** means official but incomplete, or one careful third-party measurement; **C** means secondary pages, search summaries or conflicting figures; **D** means house rules or the note-takers' own compositions.

### Rank 1: five reference files turn ui-components into a full kit

ui-components has no navigation components, no date or advanced inputs, no data-table features and no page compositions; date picker, stepper, navigation menu, segmented control, OTP, file upload, tag input, pagination as a build, KPI, pricing, sign-in, dashboard and chat bubble all have zero mentions, and the table is a head and three rows with no sort, filter, selection or pagination (`app/skills/ui-components`, grep counts in the notes). The earlier plan's combobox with chips, toggle group, sidebar, accordion and pagination did not arrive. Nearly all of it exists in shadcn's 2026 registry with exact values per style, which the notes read from the style files for both Vega (36 px controls) and Nova (32 px, the current default) ([style-vega.css](https://github.com/shadcn-ui/ui/blob/main/apps/v4/registry/styles/style-vega.css), [style-nova.css](https://github.com/shadcn-ui/ui/blob/main/apps/v4/registry/styles/style-nova.css)).

A `navigation.md` file would hold the sidebar and app shell: **256 expanded, 48 as an icon rail, 288 on mobile**, rows 32 high with a 16 icon and 8 gaps, group labels at 12 px, a sub-menu on a 1 px guide line, and a workspace row 48 high ([sidebar.tsx](https://github.com/shadcn-ui/ui/blob/main/apps/v4/registry/bases/base/ui/sidebar.tsx)); top bars of 48, 56 or 64 holding toggle, separator and title or breadcrumb; the navigation menu, pagination (links as 36 or 32 squares, the current page outlined) and the accordion. Carbon's shell agrees with shadcn exactly at a 48 header and 256 and 48 side navigation if its unit is 8 px, which the notes did not re-read ([Carbon side nav source](https://github.com/carbon-design-system/carbon/blob/main/packages/styles/scss/components/ui-shell/side-nav/_side-nav.scss)). An `inputs.md` file would hold the calendar (day cell 32 in Vega and 28 in Nova, today on a muted fill, range ends rounded only on the outer side) ([calendar.tsx](https://github.com/shadcn-ui/ui/blob/main/apps/v4/registry/bases/base/ui/calendar.tsx)), the combobox and its chips field (chip 22 high, 12 px medium), OTP slots, the input group with prefix and suffix, toggle group and segmented control, with Radix Themes' segmented control as the second measured source ([segmented-control.css](https://github.com/radix-ui/themes/blob/main/packages/radix-ui-themes/src/components/segmented-control.css)). Additions to `data.md` would cover the data-table toolbar and footer ("N of M row(s) selected", rows per page, four 32 px page buttons) and the stat tile: a 14 px muted description, a 24 px semibold figure, a delta badge and a two-line footer ([data-table.tsx](https://github.com/shadcn-ui/ui/blob/main/apps/v4/registry/bases/base/blocks/dashboard-01/components/data-table.tsx), [section-cards.tsx](https://github.com/shadcn-ui/ui/blob/main/apps/v4/registry/bases/base/blocks/dashboard-01/components/section-cards.tsx)). A `chat.md` file would carry shadcn's new assistant set: bubbles at most 80% of the thread with radius 14, the ghost variant for assistant answers, messages 32 apart, attachment chips, the trace of tool calls, the code block and the questionnaire. A `screens.md` file would carry the dashboard (288 sidebar, 48 header, 24 gutters, four KPI cards on a 16 gap, one chart card, one table) ([dashboard-01](https://github.com/shadcn-ui/ui/blob/main/apps/v4/registry/bases/base/blocks/dashboard-01/page.tsx)), sign-in in its four measured layouts (a 384 column, a split screen with a 320 form) ([login-01](https://github.com/shadcn-ui/ui/blob/main/apps/v4/registry/bases/base/blocks/login-01/page.tsx)) and the inbox master-detail at 350.

The weak part must be labelled as such inside the files. Stepper, timeline, rating, tree view, file dropzone, banner and bulk-action bar are not shadcn components; the notes compose them from the kit's tokens or from Ant Design token formulas whose default seeds were recalled from memory. Settings, pricing, checkout, onboarding, search results and error pages have no measured source at all; shadcn's registry holds only dashboard, login, signup and sidebar blocks. Sort indicators and column filters were not read at source. These should ship as "convention, built from this kit's tokens", not as specification.

### Rank 2: a charts skill, because the engine can already draw them and the guide says nothing

Charts appear in Pencraft's guide once, as a tell ("little charts ... that show nothing real", `craft.rs:171-172`), while arcs, rings, polygons, paths and line patterns are all drawable and taught by no skill (`app/src/agent.rs:37-40`). Data visualisation is a task for which 37% of surveyed creators use AI, and dashboards are a standing category on v0 and Lovable ([TechRound](https://techround.co.uk/news/canva-2026-design-predictions-what-think/), [v0 template categories](https://v0.app/templates/categories)). The anatomy is exceptionally well sourced, because shadcn, Tremor and Shopify's Polaris Viz publish it as code and agree: horizontal gridlines only, 1 px, in the border colour at half strength; no axis line and no tick marks; 12 px muted tick labels; 2 px lines; bars with 4 px corners (8 for a single wide series), stacked segments rounded only at the outer ends; a tooltip card with radius 10 and a 128 minimum width; an 8 x 8 legend swatch with 12 px labels ([chart.tsx](https://github.com/shadcn-ui/ui/blob/main/apps/v4/registry/bases/base/ui/chart.tsx), [Tremor BarChart.tsx](https://github.com/tremorlabs/tremor/blob/main/src/components/BarChart/BarChart.tsx), [Polaris Viz constants](https://github.com/Shopify/polaris-viz/blob/main/packages/polaris-viz-core/src/constants.ts)). Donut, gauge, sparkline (112 x 48, no axes), ranked bar list, uptime tracker and progress circle each have measured values in the same sources.

The skill would contain a chart-choice table, the anatomy, colour rules and a truthfulness checklist. Choice and rules come from Apple, Carbon and Datawrapper: bars and areas start at zero, lines need not; tick steps follow familiar sequences; the title states the message; comparison lines are grey and only the important ones coloured; lines are labelled directly; never rely on colour alone ([Apple HIG Charts](https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/charts), [Carbon axes and labels](https://carbondesignsystem.com/data-visualization/axes-and-labels/), [Datawrapper](https://www.datawrapper.de/blog/line-charts)). Categorical palettes are published with hex values by Carbon (14 colours ordered for neighbour contrast, plus sequential and diverging sets) and by Atlassian (eight per theme) ([Carbon colour palettes](https://carbondesignsystem.com/data-visualization/color-palettes/), [Atlassian tokens](https://unpkg.com/@atlaskit/tokens/dist/esm/artifacts/tokens-raw/atlassian-light.js)). The checklist is what makes a mock-up chart believable: every number shown twice agrees (donut centre equals the sum of slices, the delta matches the two values it compares), bar heights are computed from values, data has dips and noise in the way shadcn's own samples do (186, 305, 237, 73, 209, 214) ([chart-bar-multiple.tsx](https://github.com/shadcn-ui/ui/blob/main/apps/v4/registry/new-york-v4/charts/chart-bar-multiple.tsx)). The checklist and the sample-data recipe are the note-taker's derivations; no source on fake-looking mock-up charts was found. Unsourced gaps to mark: bar-to-gap ratio, scatter, heatmap and funnel anatomy, and number formatting rules. The open `data-and-numbers` skill from the earlier plan folds in here as one reference file on figures.

### Rank 3: a formats skill of sizes and safe zones

Social graphics are the most common AI design task in the one usable survey (69% of 1,000 creators), and nothing in Pencraft's guide or skills mentions social graphics, banners, business cards, slides or email ([TechRound](https://techround.co.uk/news/canva-2026-design-predictions-what-think/)). This family fails on dimensions, not taste, and the dimensions changed recently: Instagram's profile grid has shown 3:4 thumbnails since January 2025, so the default feed canvas is **1080 x 1350** with everything important inside the centred 1012-wide strip ([Sked Social](https://skedsocial.com/blog/best-instagram-image-and-video-size-recommendations.md), [Sprout Social](https://sproutsocial.com/insights/social-media-image-sizes-guide/)). The skill is a lookup table per surface plus a handful of rules, and its quality varies by file.

The store file is the strongest, read from official pages: App Store screenshots at 1320 x 2868 for the large iPhone class and 2064 x 2752 for the 13-inch iPad, with no transparency ([App Store Connect Help](https://developer.apple.com/help/app-store-connect/reference/app-information/screenshot-specifications)); Google Play's 512 x 512 icon and 1024 x 500 feature graphic ([Play Console Help](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/9866151)); Steam's capsules, enlarged in August 2024 with the old sizes rejected ([Steamworks](https://partner.steamgames.com/doc/store/assets)); the Chrome Web Store's 1280 x 800 screenshots and 440 x 280 tile ([Chrome for Developers](https://developer.chrome.com/docs/webstore/images)); GitHub's 1280 x 640 social preview and the favicon set. One conflict stands: which iPhone screenshot class is formally required. Google's ad sizes, YouTube's 3840 x 2160 thumbnail and Apple Podcasts' 3000 x 3000 cover are also official ([Google Ads Help](https://support.google.com/google-ads/answer/17140672), [YouTube Help](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/72431)).

The social file is weaker and must say so. All Meta figures are third-party; X post sizes conflict (1600 x 900 against 1200 x 675, both 16:9); and safe zones for 9:16 disagree by source. The most conservative verified set is TikTok's own template measured at 240 top, 660 bottom and 120 each side on 1080 x 1920 ([ManyPixels](https://www.manypixels.co/blog/social-media-design/tiktok-ad-specs)), and the Meta-derived Instagram zone is 14% top, 35% bottom and 6% sides ([Sked Social](https://skedsocial.com/blog/best-instagram-image-and-video-size-recommendations.md)). Drawing to the larger margin of the two is a defensible house rule. 1200 x 630 for link previews is a convention, not part of any protocol. No reliable source gave minimum text sizes for a 1080 canvas.

Print and email are further reference files in the same skill. Paper sizes are stable and sourced ([Wikipedia, Paper size](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paper_size)); regional business cards and a full bleed, trim and safe example come from one printer ([Vistaprint](https://www.vistaprint.com/hub/business-card-dimensions)). The working rules (3 mm bleed, content 3 to 5 mm inside the trim, 6 pt minimum type and 8 pt when reversed, 0.25 pt minimum line) come from printers' pages seen through search summaries and differ by printer, so the skill should name the printer's own template as the authority ([BoxMart artwork specification](https://cdn.shopify.com/s/files/1/0263/0788/7138/files/BoxMart_Artwork_Specification_2023.pdf)). Email reduces, for a picture of an email, to a 600 px single column, a dark-mode sibling frame (more than a third of subscribers read in dark mode) and a visible footer; button and type sizes are vendor-blog numbers that disagree ([Litmus](https://www.litmus.com/blog/email-design-best-practices)). Resumes, flyers with folds, book covers and labels were not researched.

### Rank 4: Material 3 Expressive, the best-sourced platform

Android holds 44.56% of all-platform traffic in October 2026, and Google says its Material kit is used ten times more than the next most popular kit, a vendor's own claim ([StatCounter](https://gs.statcounter.com/os-market-share/all/worldwide/2012), [Google Design](https://design.google/library/euphrates-dahout-material-design-figma)). The whole foundation can be drawn from the Jetpack Compose token files, which the notes read directly ([androidx Material 3 tokens](https://github.com/androidx/androidx/tree/androidx-main/compose/material3/material3/src/commonMain/kotlin/androidx/compose/material3/tokens)): colour roles as tone numbers for light and dark with baseline hex values; **15 type roles plus 15 emphasized twins**; a ten-step corner scale with the new 20, 32 and 48 steps; six elevation levels; state-layer opacities of 8, 10, 10 and 16%; and a component table covering some 45 components. The Expressive values a model's memory is most likely to get wrong are the five button sizes (32, 40, 56, 96, 136), the 64-high navigation bar (the older one was 80), flexible app bars, the floating 64 pill toolbar, split buttons and button groups with 2 px gaps, and the thick 16 px slider. Window size classes (600, 840, 1200, 1600) are official ([Android Developers](https://developer.android.com/develop/ui/compose/layouts/adaptive/use-window-size-classes)).

Three things are missing and should be marked. Android publishes no heights for the status and navigation bars, only that they are transparent and edge to edge ([Android Developers, System bars](https://developer.android.com/design/ui/mobile/guides/foundations/system-bars)). Paddings that live only on the specification pages could not be read. Deriving a palette from a seed colour is an algorithm the notes did not read at source, so the skill should ship the tone table and a few precomputed palettes. The Android device template belongs in devices, using the Pixel numbers that skill already states in prose.

### Rank 5: guide sections for colour ramps, stock fonts and the missing vocabulary

Anthropic reports typography as the single most effective lever and committed palettes as the next, on side-by-side screenshots without metrics ([Anthropic blog](https://claude.com/blog/improving-frontend-design-through-skills)). Pencraft's guide stops at six colour roles and lists installed fonts without saying which are good or how they pair. Two additions are well sourced. The first is a ramp method: each hue as an 11 or 12 step ramp with one job per step (backgrounds, component fills at rest, hover and pressed, three border strengths, the solid and its hover, two text steps) ([Radix Colors](https://www.radix-ui.com/colors/docs/palette-composition/understanding-the-scale)); lightness targets lifted from Tailwind's published OKLCH ramps, with chroma peaking in the middle and falling toward both ends ([Tailwind CSS docs](https://tailwindcss.com/docs/colors)); greys tinted toward the accent by Radix's hue-matching table; dark themes built by reusing the roles on a reversed ramp, never by inverting. WCAG 2 ratios stay the only normative contrast test: APCA was removed from the WCAG 3 draft in 2023 and the April 2026 draft has no chosen algorithm, so APCA is at most an advisory second number ([Adrian Roselli](https://adrianroselli.com/2026/04/wcag3-contrast-as-of-april-2026.html)). Pencraft's existing text-contrast thresholds therefore need no correction.

The second is a pairing table built from fonts that are actually installed. Microsoft and Apple document their inventories: Windows 11 ships Sitka in six optical sizes, Segoe UI Variable in three, Bahnschrift with a width axis and Cascadia; macOS ships Avenir Next, Charter, Baskerville, Didot, Futura, Optima and Gill Sans ([Microsoft Learn](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/typography/fonts/windows_11_font_list), [Apple Support](https://support.apple.com/en-us/120414)). Availability is grade A (the Windows list is dated April 2022); the quality ranking is one typographer's opinion ([Practical Typography](https://practicaltypography.com/system-fonts.html)); the pairings themselves (Sitka Display with Segoe UI Variable Text, Avenir Next with Charter) are the note-taker's suggestions. Body-text rules are firmly sourced: 15 to 25 px on screen, line spacing 120 to 145%, 45 to 90 characters a line, capitals tracked 5 to 12% ([Practical Typography](https://practicaltypography.com/summary-of-key-rules.html)). The vocabulary gap from the corrections section is filled here too: "denser" as one step down the control ladder, "dark mode" as added Mode values, "accessible" as the checks under rank 6, "options" as three named directions differing on two structural axes.

### Rank 6: checks, because the field has moved from prose to verification

Impeccable ships 62 deterministic detector rules, the DESIGN.md tooling lints contrast, and Claude Design checks output against the imported system before showing it ([Impeccable README](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/pbakaus/impeccable/main/README.md), [VentureBeat](https://venturebeat.com/technology/anthropic-ships-major-claude-design-overhaul-with-design-system-imports-code-round-trips-and-a-fix-for-its-token-burning-problem)). Pencraft's canvas is structured data, so it is well placed for this, yet almost all the P1 checks planned on 14 November are still open: unequal control heights in a row, the control ladder, side padding of a control, icon discipline, target size, shadow vocabulary, heavy lines, dark-theme edges, contrast in both themes, mixed decimals in figure columns, and concentric radii outside glass (`app/MECHANICS.md:260`). The art-director review is still unavailable to outside clients (`app/src/agent.rs` tool list).

New checks follow from WCAG 2.2, read at the specification: the large-text exception (3:1 from 18 pt, or 14 pt bold); 3:1 for state indicators, focus rings and chart marks; targets of at least 24 x 24, with 44 x 44 advised on touch frames; and a heuristic for meaning carried by colour alone, such as an error field with a red border and no icon or message ([WCAG 2.2](https://www.w3.org/TR/WCAG22/), [Understanding 2.5.8](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Understanding/target-size-minimum.html)). The earlier study's caution still holds: applying 3:1 to ordinary control borders would fail every library studied, so the rule should bind only checked, selected and focus indicators. Each new skill should bring its own switch in the manner of `check_glass` and the logo counts: for charts, sums that agree and bars on a zero baseline; for formats, a frame that matches a known size and text inside the safe zone; for icons, the stroke and padding counts under rank 12. All thresholds are proposals to tune against the nine review cases, which still include no component sheet, chart, diagram, slide or social graphic.

### Ranks 7 and 8: landing pages and slides fail on craft, not dimensions

Landing pages and posters are excluded by name from ui-components and logos, and Pencil, the closest benchmark, ships guideline topics for landing page, mobile app, web app, slides, table and design system, none of which maps onto Pencraft's four skills; that list comes from an undated third-party guide ([Lullabot guide](https://lullabot.github.io/prompt_library/design/skills/pencil-designer/references/mcp-tools-guide.md)). A `landing-pages` skill would hold the verified width scale (a 1280 content container, 1152 one step down, breakpoints at 640, 768, 1024, 1280 and 1536) ([Tailwind CSS docs](https://tailwindcss.com/docs/responsive-design)); six hero structures; and research-based rules that translate directly into drawing rules: logo top left, a tagline that says what the company does, button labels that say what the user gets and never "Learn more", real product images, no false floor ([NN/g](https://www.nngroup.com/articles/homepage-design-principles/)). It should ask for the buyer, the strongest proof and the real product picture before drawing, since the 2026 critiques turn on content. Its numbers are the thin part: section padding and headline sizes come from unofficial copies of licensed blocks, the common 1440 and 390 frame widths were not found in any citable source, and the pricing defaults (three or four tiers, one highlighted) rest on a study known only through a summary ([HubSpot](https://blog.hubspot.com/marketing/best-practices-pricing-page)).

Presentations are the third most common AI design task (49%) and Claude Design, Pencil and Anthropic's pptx skill all treat them as first-class ([TechRound](https://techround.co.uk/news/canva-2026-design-predictions-what-think/), [pptx SKILL.md](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/anthropics/skills/main/skills/pptx/SKILL.md)). The sources agree on behaviour: one idea per slide, the title as a full-sentence conclusion placed near the top, large bold text, no screenshots dictating type size ([Y Combinator](https://www.ycombinator.com/blog/how-to-design-a-better-pitch-deck)); pitch-deck orders from YC and Sequoia; and Anthropic's seven layout options and avoid-list. Numbers are few: a 24-point accessibility floor and Kawasaki's 30-point minimum, both seen only second-hand ([UGA accessible slides](https://dash.uga.edu/create-accessible-content/slides/)); converting points to pixels on a 1920 x 1080 frame is the note-taker's arithmetic; no source gives margins or safe areas. The skill should separate a presented deck from a reading deck, because the size rules differ.

### Ranks 9 to 16: useful, but smaller, weaker or blocked

A design-system document comes ninth. Stitch, Claude Design, Figma Make, Subframe and Impeccable all keep one persistent document per project holding tokens, component rules and do and don't lists, and check output against it; Google's DESIGN.md specification, still alpha, fixes token groups and ten ordered sections ([design.md README](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/google-labs-code/design.md/main/README.md)). For Pencraft this is a guide section on reading such a file into one `set_variables` call and treating it as a named reference with numbers, a brand-board template (a public brand-kit skill ranks in the top twenty by installs) ([claudemarketplaces ranking](https://claudemarketplaces.com/best/skills/design-ui)), and the still-open record of accepted decisions. One caution: a Figma user reported guidelines followed while an existing component went unused, so a document alone does not guarantee reuse ([Figma forum](https://forum.figma.com/report-a-problem-6/make-kit-feature-55860)).

`windows-fluent` is tenth despite Windows' 76.65% desktop share, because requests for it are probably rarer than its share suggests; the notes have no demand data for it ([StatCounter](https://gs.statcounter.com/os-market-share/desktop/worldwide)). Its numbers are grade A from Microsoft's documentation and token source: a nine-style type ramp from 12/16 to 68/92 in Regular and Semibold only, radii of 4 for controls and 8 for windows and overlays, a 32 px title bar (48 with search), breakpoints at 640 and 1007, shadow recipes, a grey ramp and a brand ramp ([Microsoft Learn, Typography](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/windows/apps/design/signature-experiences/typography), [Geometry](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/windows/apps/design/signature-experiences/geometry), [fluentui tokens](https://github.com/microsoft/fluentui/blob/master/packages/tokens/src/utils/shadows.ts)). Control heights and caption-button sizes were not on the pages read. Material and Fluent should be separate skills, not one "platforms" skill, because each needs its own tokens file and the existing document test takes one skill with one tokens file (`app/src/skills.rs:319-376`).

`diagrams` is eleventh. Connectors are taught by the format guide and used by no skill (`app/src/agent.rs:41-62`), so the engine is ahead of the library. Conventions are sourced: three flowchart shapes, one direction, every decision exit labelled, wireflow arrows that start at the control acted on ([NN/g Wireflows](https://www.nngroup.com/articles/wireflows/)); the C4 checklist of title, legend, typed elements and verb-phrase arrows ([C4 model](https://c4model.com/diagrams/notation)); fixed row layouts for journey maps and service blueprints ([NN/g](https://www.nngroup.com/articles/service-blueprints-definition/)). Wireframes belong here as a fidelity mode. No authoritative source gives node gaps, arrowhead sizes or line weights; every number would be a house rule.

`icons` is twelfth and unusually checkable. Lucide, the set Pencraft ships, publishes its recipe: a 24 canvas, at least 1 px padding, a 2 px centred stroke never mixed, round caps and joins, 2 px corner radius, 2 px minimum gaps ([Lucide icon design guide](https://lucide.dev/guide/design/icon-design-guide)); Tabler matches it nearly number for number. A drawn icon either meets those counts or does not. Keyline sizes for Lucide are unpublished, and the list of typical faults in generated icons is inference.

Apple beyond glass is thirteenth because the full skill is blocked on data. Apple publishes exact type tables, widget and Live Activity sizes and the layered 1024 x 1024 icon canvas with six appearances ([HIG Typography](https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/typography), [HIG Widgets](https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/widgets), [HIG App icons](https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/app-icons)), and those can ship now as reference files. It publishes no bar heights, list row heights or macOS window measurements in readable form, lists system colours as swatch images whose values changed on 9 June 2025, and its widget table has no rows for the current 402, 420 and 440 point phones ([HIG Color](https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/color)). A plain iOS list, navigation bar or settings screen has to be measured from Apple's design kits first.

`illustration` is fourteenth although 40% of creators use AI for it, because the public knowledge is thin: Atlassian's types (spot, low-fidelity interface, ambient pattern, hero) and its "is it needed at all" gate ([Atlassian Design](https://atlassian.design/foundations/illustrations)); empty-state content rules ([NN/g](https://www.nngroup.com/articles/empty-state-interface-design/)); and the record that the flat long-limbed corporate style is treated as overused ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Corporate_Memphis)). Palette counts, proportions and sizes are house rules, and no public agent skill addresses illustration, so there is nothing to benchmark against. The skill should also say when to switch from shapes to the generated-picture tool, which no skill mentions today.

Fifteenth are three items still open from the earlier plan: worst-case frames (long names, empty, one, many), three named variants side by side, and the opt-in effects recipes with a budget. Sixteenth are short tables for television (tvOS insets of 60 and 80 points, Android TV's 960 x 540 canvas), CarPlay frame sizes, watch type sizes and visionOS windows, all official but rarely requested ([HIG Layout](https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/layout), [Android Developers, TV layouts](https://developer.android.com/design/ui/tv/guides/styles/layouts)).

Several things in the notes are left out because Pencraft draws still pictures: motion skills, which make up a large share of public design skills, code-stack and performance guidance, image-to-code, email weight limits and responsive behaviour beyond a second frame at another width. A large style and palette table keyed by product type, in the manner of UI UX Pro Max's 194 palettes, is also not recommended: no evidence shows such tables beat short prose, and Pencraft's dealt worlds already supply variety ([UI UX Pro Max README](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/nextlevelbuilder/ui-ux-pro-max-skill/main/README.md)).

## Engine gaps and licences limit eleven candidates

None of the limits below stops a skill from shipping; each changes what the skill must say or how faithful the result can be. The engine facts are from `app/MECHANICS.md:305-310` and `app/skills/ui-components/SKILL.md:190-200` unless another source is given.

| Limit | What it touches | Consequence for the library |
|---|---|---|
| No OpenType features, so no tabular figures | Charts, tables, stat tiles, anything in a figure column | Skills must name families whose figures are one width; the figure-column check stays advisory |
| No truncation, maximum lines, minimum or maximum size, or wrapping layout | Chips fields, table cells, list previews clamped to two lines, responsive grids | Truncation drawn by hand; a second width is a second frame |
| Dashed strokes only on connectors | File dropzones, projection lines in charts, blueprint lines, wireframes | Solid quiet lines, or a connector where a straight dashed rule is enough |
| Icons from Lucide only, with no icon weight | Material Symbols, SF Symbols, Segoe Fluent Icons; shadcn styles that use other sets | Platform skills must describe Lucide stand-ins and admit the difference; Lucide has no filled forms for selected tabs |
| No blend modes | Windows Acrylic's exclusion layer, screen-blended light in effects recipes | Acrylic drawn as blur plus tint; noted as approximate |
| No corner smoothing | Apple icon masks and large Apple radii | Plain rounded rectangles; noted in liquid-glass already |
| PDF is one page sized to the layers, with no page, bleed or presentation concept | Print pieces, decks, carousels | Bleed drawn into the frame; a deck is frames side by side, exported one file per selection; no slide-file export, which Claude Design offers |
| Colours stored as hex; `oklch()` input unverified | Ramp method, shadcn and Tailwind tokens | Ramps computed outside and shipped as hex, conversions marked approximate |
| No motion, no live browser nodes, no code export | Effects, prototypes | Out of scope by design |
| Apple's San Francisco fonts | Apple skills and templates | Mock-ups of Apple-platform software only, registered developers only, no embedding, no redistribution ([Apple Developer, Fonts](https://developer.apple.com/fonts/)); request by family name with an open fallback |
| Microsoft's Segoe fonts | `windows-fluent` | No redistribution or embedding in apps; images made with them are allowed; Segoe UI Variable is not licensable off Windows; Selawik is Microsoft's named open substitute ([Microsoft Learn, font FAQ](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/typography/fonts/font-faq)) |

Three licensing points deserve a decision by a person, and none of this is legal advice. Pencraft's PDF export embeds fonts (`app/MECHANICS.md:181-185`), and Apple's terms say the font may not be embedded "in any software programs or other products"; whether an exported PDF mock-up falls under that was not researched. The licences for SF Symbols, Roboto, Material Symbols and Segoe Fluent Icons were not fetched; Google Sans Flex is confirmed open under the SIL licence since November 2025 ([Android Authority](https://www.androidauthority.com/google-sans-flex-free-3617034/)). And measured anatomy from licensed block libraries cannot be the basis of a landing-page skill: the Tailwind Plus values in the notes come from unofficial copies, and Aceternity's licence forbids redistributing its items as a kit ([Aceternity licence](https://ui.aceternity.com/licence)). Teaching general recipes from open sources is a different act from bundling a recreated kit, and the skills should stay on the first side of that line.

Two limits are about data, not engine or law. Apple's and Android's unpublished bar heights mean any figure in a skill is a measurement to label and date. And the templates the list implies (Android phone, iPad, desktop window, browser frame, slide, social frames with safe-zone guides, a print frame with bleed) vary in footing: the Windows window is specified, the Android and iPad bodies are already in devices as prose, but browser chrome has no official specification at all, so a browser frame should be a neutral two-grey drawing in the manner of Magic UI's mock-ups ([safari.json](https://magicui.design/r/safari.json)), not an imitation of a named browser.

## Conclusion

The earlier study concluded that Pencraft's assistant needed permission to be conventional. This one shows where convention has to be supplied as data. The additions sort less by subject than by kind of knowledge: tables a model cannot derive (formats, platform tokens, measured anatomy), rules that can be counted (chart sums, icon strokes, target sizes), and judgement that can only be described (illustration, landing-page argument, slide titles). Pencraft's machinery suits the first two kinds far better than the third: skill files are compiled in, their examples are reviewed in both themes, and the canvas is structured enough for counted checks. That is the reason to do reference files and checks before craft prose, and it is also why the corrections come first: a library whose own tests pass while its text denies engine features has drifted in exactly the way tables drift.

Perishability is the standing cost. shadcn changed its default style within a year, Instagram its grid in 2025, Steam its capsules in 2024, Apple its colours in 2025 and its glass again in 2026. Every stored number should carry its source and date, conflicting figures should be shipped as conflicts with the conservative choice named, and unsourced compositions should be labelled as house rules. The untested part is the ranking itself. The cheapest way to test it is the one already in the repository: write the first three items, run fresh-agent scenarios for a dashboard with a chart, an Instagram carousel and a Material settings screen, and let the friction reports reorder the rest.
